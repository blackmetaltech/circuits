Controls (workbench)

Left click a tray tile, then a padPlace the part
RTurn the part before you place it
W, then two padsRun a wire
Right click a part or a wireRemove it
SpacePower on or off
EnterBench test
MMeter leads (red, then black)
PScope probe
TabSchematic
Ctrl+ZUndo
1 to 5Camera views (5 looks straight down on the board)
Right drag / middle drag / wheelOrbit / pan / zoom
EscSelect tool
Wheel over a part on the boardChange its value in place (the wires stay)
Wheel over the sorting boxPick another value of that part
Wheel over the VOLTAGE knobSet the bench supply
Left and Right, or the wheel over the slide wireMove the jockey (J changes the step)
KTestbed: pause or run the simulation
LTestbed: one frame forward while paused

Gamepad

Left stickMove the cursor ring (it slows over anything it can act on)
A / BClick / right click at the ring
LB / RBWheel down / up at the ring
XPower
YBench test
D-pad up / downWire tool / meter leads
D-pad left / rightCamera views
BackSchematic
StartSelect tool / menu
Right stickOrbit the view
Left stick pressTestbed: pause or run
Right stick pressTestbed: step; on a job: the jockey step size

The pad rumbles on power, on a pass and on a burn. The classic stall has its own controls on its help page.
